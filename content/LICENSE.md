## Readings (`content/readings/`)

The Bible text is the World English Bible, which is in the public domain.
The choice of readings for each day follows the Lectionary for Mass used in
the United States. The citations come from
[cpbjr/catholic-readings-api](https://github.com/cpbjr/catholic-readings-api)
(MIT license), with corrections in `scripts/readings/overrides.json`.
