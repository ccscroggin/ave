How reviewing works

One month at a time

  1. About six weeks ahead, I send the drafts for the next month’s Sundays and holy days: Plain, Kids and Family for each day.
  2. You read them on paper or on your phone at ave-daily.vercel.app/review, where you can fix wording in place and approve with one tap.
  3. Mark anything to change, or reject a text. Return the pages or reply by email.
  4. I make the changes and record your approval with your name and the date.
  5. Only then does the guide appear in the app and in printed booklets.

What to look for

  • Faithful to the Church’s teaching and to how the Church reads the passage.
  • No mistakes of fact: names, places, dates, background.
  • The plain-words version adds nothing to the reading and leaves out nothing important.
  • Kids text is honest about hard parts, gentle, and not babyish.
  • Nothing a parent or catechist would object to.

Reminders

Your personal review link has a Reminders button. One tap puts each month’s deadline in your phone’s calendar, with an alert a week before and a day before. Tapping the alert opens your review page.

Sharing the work

Several priests can review together. Each has a personal link, with no password. Anyone can claim a Sunday so two people do not review the same day, and everyone can see who has which day. One approval is enough for an edition to appear in the app, and every decision stays on record.

Your name in the app

Approved guides can read “Guide reviewed by” with your name, or show no name. Your choice.

Lines the drafts flag for a careful look in Advent

Nov 29. The first reading jumps from Isaiah 63:19b to 64:2, as the Lectionary does.

Dec 8, Immaculate Conception. Each edition says the feast is Mary’s own conception free from original sin, by grace through her Son, and that the Annunciation is about the conception of Jesus. Genesis 3:15 is explained as the first announcement of the Savior, with Mary as the new Eve (following the Catechism, 410–411). The angel’s greeting reads “full of grace.”

Dec 13. John 1:19 “the Jews” is rendered “the Jewish leaders in Jerusalem.”

Dec 20. 2 Samuel 7:14 is restated as discipline, as a parent disciplines a child. The kids text keeps the word “virgin” without further explanation.
