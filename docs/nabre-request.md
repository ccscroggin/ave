# NABRE license request (draft, not sent)

To: nabperm@usccb.org
Subject: License request: Lectionary readings in a print-on-demand missalette and app

Dear Permissions Office,

I am building Ave, a free web app that helps Catholics pray the rosary
and prepare for Mass. It includes a small printed missalette that pairs
the Sunday and holy day readings with a short explanation in everyday
words, in three editions: one for adults, one for children aged 6 to 11,
and one for families to talk through together. A priest or catechist
reviews each explanation before it is published.

Today the app uses the World English Bible, which is in the public
domain. Because people bring the booklet to Mass, I would like to use
the Lectionary for Mass texts (NABRE) so the words match what they hear.

I am asking for a license covering:

1. Display of the daily Mass readings in the app (web, free, no ads).
2. Free print-at-home booklets downloaded from the app.
3. Printed booklets sold by subscription and printed on demand, one copy
   per order. I expect fewer than 500 copies a month in the first year.
   After printing, shipping and card fees, profits go to Catholic
   charities.

Please tell me the terms, fees and any review you require. I am glad to
send sample pages.

Thank you,
Clark Scroggin
