# Code of conduct

Ave is a Catholic project, and its contributors are asked to work in the
spirit of charity.

## What we ask

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their background, role or
  experience. A first-time contributor and a bishop get the same courtesy.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Questions of doctrine are
  settled by the teaching of the Church, not by who argues longest.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you accuse.
- Keep discussion on the work in front of us.
- Protect people’s privacy. Do not share anyone’s personal information.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Mocking anyone’s faith, including people who are not Catholic.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Sharing someone’s private information without permission.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, @ccscroggin, through GitHub
(use “Report content” on the comment, or a private security advisory for
anything sensitive). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit
or remove comments, and may block anyone who will not follow this code.
